# Hackathon Presentation & Live Demo Plan
**Smart Waste Intelligence Platform (SWIP)**

## 1. 30-Second Opening Hook & Pitch

> *"Judges, current municipal waste systems are broken: cities either run costly fixed routes emptying half-empty bins, or wait for angry citizens to complain about overflowing garbage.  
> We built the **Smart Waste Intelligence Platform**.  
> **We don't just report garbage. We detect it, predict it, dispatch the right resource, verify collection, and learn from the data.**  
> Today, we demonstrate a complete, closed-loop operational system featuring a virtual IoT digital twin, automated AI dispatch, citizen engagement, and verified worker resolution."*

---

## 2. The 5 Screens to Walk Judges Through

### Screen A: Admin Command Center
- **What to show**: 12 critical bins, 27 open complaints, 84 pickups today, 5 hotspots.
- **Key story**: The centralized dashboard gives city sanitation chiefs total operational visibility across bins, citizen tickets, and collection fleets in real time.

### Screen B: Smart Bin Monitoring (IoT Digital Twin)
- **What to show**: Fleet of smart bins (B-101 to B-112) with live fill levels, temperatures, and weights.
- **Key story**: Emphasize transparency — this is an authentic software-first digital twin simulating real sensor telemetry.
- **Interactive feature**: The built-in **"Simulate Sensor Data"** panel.

### Screen C: The Live IoT Simulation Trigger (The "Magic Moment")
1. Open the simulator panel for **Bin B-102**.
2. Slide fill level to **95%** (or click preset *"Simulate Overflow Surge"*).
3. Click **"Publish Sensor Reading"**.
4. Observe the immediate reactive event: Bin B-102 turns glowing red (`CRITICAL`), AI score jumps to 94, and an automated collection task is spawned instantly without any citizen complaint needed.

### Screen D: AI Operations & Dispatch
- **What to show**: Multi-factor priority list, route order recommendations, and automated assignment to **Worker Rahul S. (Vehicle #04)**.
- **Key story**: AI evaluates fill rate + location risk + complaint density to allocate resources where they matter most.

### Screen E: Worker Task View & Verified Resolution
- **What to show**: Switch to Worker View — Rahul sees his newly assigned critical task with map navigation.
- **Action**: Worker clicks `Accept Task` → `Start Route` → `Arrived` → `Upload Proof & Complete`.
- **Closed Loop Confirmation**: The proof is submitted, the ticket closes, Bin B-102 resets to **18% fill**, and Admin KPIs update dynamically!
